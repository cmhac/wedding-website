Set up email sending

Sign-in links, invitations and the save the date go out by email. This is set in your hosting account, not here, so a secret never passes through this page.

  1. Open your hosting project, then Settings, Environment Variables.
  2. Add RESEND_API_KEY (from your email provider) and EMAIL_FROM, an address on your verified domain.
  3. Redeploy the site, then choose Check again.
